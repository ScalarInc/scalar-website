About

Senior engineers who stay for the hard part

Helios is a small studio in Perth. We work with organisations whose systems have real physical consequences — grain moving, trucks rolling, plant running.

Who we are

Deliberately small.

We kept the team small on purpose. Every engagement is led by an engineer with a decade or more behind them, and that person stays on the work from the first conversation through to handover.

It means we take on fewer projects than we could. It also means nobody on your project is learning on your budget.

Our clients are mostly in agriculture, resources, logistics and the public sector — industries where a system going down is not an inconvenience, it's a truck queue.

Principles

What we hold to.

01

Say the hard thing early

A bad estimate delivered gently is still a bad estimate. We'd rather have the awkward conversation in week one.

02

Build for the tenth year

Most of the systems we've written are still running. That changes how you choose dependencies.

03

Leave it handed over

Documentation, runbooks and a team that can operate it without us. Lock-in is not a business model.

Certification

Security, independently audited.

We hold ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification for information security management. For clients in regulated industries, that means our controls have been examined by someone other than us.
