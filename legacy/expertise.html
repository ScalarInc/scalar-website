Expertise

What we are actually good at

Four disciplines that tend to arrive together: someone has to hold the technical line, design the system, connect it to everything else, and know when a model beats a rule.

01

Technical leadership

Fractional CTO and lead engineer cover for teams that have outgrown their first build but aren't ready to hire a permanent architect.

02

System architecture

Designing systems that survive contact with real operations — clear boundaries, honest failure modes, and a migration path off whatever you're on now.

03

Integration

Getting legacy plant systems, ERPs, field hardware and modern services to agree on what happened, and when, and in what order.

04

Applied machine learning

Forecasting, classification and anomaly detection where the data is thin and the cost of a confident wrong answer is high.

05

Platform engineering

Build pipelines, observability and environments that let a small team ship on a Friday without anyone losing a weekend.

06

Technical due diligence

An unsentimental read on someone else's codebase before you buy it, invest in it, or agree to maintain it.

Engagement

How it starts

Most work begins with a short, paid discovery — usually two to four weeks. You get a written assessment, an architecture you can act on, and a delivery estimate you can take to a board.

If the right answer is "don't build this", we'll say so in that document. It has happened more than once and it has never cost us a client.

  • Discovery and technical assessment
  • Architecture and delivery planning
  • Build, with a senior engineer accountable end to end
  • Support, monitoring and structured handover
